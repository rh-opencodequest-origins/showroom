== Your team

You are a team of *3 people* working together with *2 roles*:

* *Platform Engineer* -- sets up standards, integrations, and golden paths
* *Developer* -- consumes the platform to build, deploy, and run applications

Some parts are *shared* (one person drives, others follow), some are *individual*. You will explore 3 topics:

. Design and deploy an Internal Developer Platform with {product_name_rhdh}
. Develop and deploy microservices using the platform you built
. Iterate and improve the platform
