== Objectives

* Understand the challenges developers face and why an Internal Developer Platform matters
* Discover {product_name_rhdh} and how it helps teams onboard and ship faster
* Get ready for the hands-on labs ahead

== The scenario

Open Code Quest Organization is a superhero developer company. The team is growing fast but working in silos, which causes:

* Difficulty knowing who is doing what
* Slow onboarding of new team members
* No reuse of existing artifacts
* No developer self-service

The solution: an *Internal Developer Platform (IDP)* built with *{product_name_rhdh}*.

== What is {product_name_rhdh}?

{product_name_rhdh} is a unified, open platform that reduces cognitive load for developers. It extends the https://backstage.io/[Backstage^] project with OpenShift integration, enterprise RBAC, and dynamic plugins.

== What you will do in this workshop

. *Design* an Internal Developer Platform -- integrate SSO (Keycloak), SCM (GitLab), and GitOps (Argo CD) with {product_name_rhdh}
. *Build* software templates and golden paths for your team
. *Develop* microservices using the platform you created
. *Iterate* and improve the platform with monitoring, observability, and orchestration
